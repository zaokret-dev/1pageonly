The Shop

Two generations, one workbench.

Walt started framing houses in the nineties. His daughter Rosie joined after trade school, and together they turned a garage workshop in Sellwood into the business it is today.

We keep the crew small on purpose. The person who measures your space is the person who cuts the wood and drives the last screw, so nothing gets lost between a salesman and an installer.

— Walt & Rosie
