After the Storm

Signs your roof needs a look.

Hail bruises and lifted shingles are easy to miss from the driveway. Left alone, they let water creep under the deck and into insulation — damage that rarely shows up until months later.

If you notice any of these, call before your insurer's filing window closes.

  • Granules piling up in guttersshingles losing their protective layer
  • Dents on vents, flashing or the AC unita reliable clue that hail struck the roof too
  • Stains on upstairs ceilingswater is already finding a path inside
  • Shingles in the yard after high windsexposed underlayment needs covering fast
  • Daylight visible in the atticgaps around chimneys, vents or the ridge
