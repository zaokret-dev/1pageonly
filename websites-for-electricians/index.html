FAQ for Electricians

Questions From
Electrical Contractors.

Can my license number and insurance be shown on the site?+

Yes, and we recommend it. Your license number, insurance and any certifications go near the top of the page and again near the contact section. For electrical work, proof of licensing is often what decides whether a homeowner calls you or keeps scrolling.

I want more panel upgrades and EV charger installs. Can the site push those?+

Absolutely. We give your most profitable services their own highlighted spots with clear descriptions, so customers searching for those specific jobs see right away that you do them — instead of a long list where everything looks the same.

I do both residential and commercial work. Can one website cover both?+

Yes. We organize the page so homeowners and business owners each find their services quickly. The Pro + Local SEO plan gives you up to 3 pages, which is useful if you want a separate page for commercial clients.

How will homeowners find me on Google?+

In two ways. Your website is written around the electrical jobs people actually search for in your area, which helps it appear in the regular results. The map listings — the businesses shown next to the map — depend mostly on your Google Business Profile, its reviews and how close you are to the person searching, and setting that profile up properly is available as an optional add-on. Rankings take time and nobody can promise first place, but you start on a solid foundation.

What kind of photos work best for an electrician?+

Clean, finished work: a tidy panel, a neatly installed EV charger, your branded van, you and your crew on the job. Photos taken on a phone are fine. If you don't have any yet, we start with quality stock photos and replace them when you're ready.

Do I need a separate website for generator or solar work?+

Usually not. If those services are a smaller part of your business, they fit well as their own sections on the page. With the Pro plan you get up to 3 pages, so a bigger specialty like generator installations can have a page of its own.
