Rooms

What we transform.

i.

Kitchens

Layouts reworked around how you actually cook, with custom cabinetry, quartz or soapstone tops and lighting that flatters the food.

ii.

Bathrooms

Curbless showers, heated floors and tile set by hand — from tight powder rooms to calm primary suites.

iii.

Lower Levels

Dry, warm basements finished as guest suites, home offices or a family room everyone wants to use in February.

iv.

Whole-Floor Renovations

Walls opened, floors replaced and old houses brought up to code without losing the details that made you buy them.
